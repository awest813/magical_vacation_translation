# Translation Style Guide

## General rules

- **Item and place names**: Capitalize the first letter of each word (title case).
- **Ellipses**: Always three periods — `...` never more or fewer.
- **Exclamation/question combo**: Use `?!`, not `!?`.
- **Character names in dialogue boxes**: Use a colon after the name for clarity (e.g. `Mokka: ...`). Quotation marks are not preferred since they are not consistently closed in the engine.
- **Profanity**: Keep the game E-rated. Do not use "crap", "hell", "damn", "shit", or stronger language.
- **Emphasis**: Words can be ALL CAPS to function like italics or bold (the engine does not support those styles).
- **Pronouns for spirits**: Use "it", not he/she.

## Character-specific notes

- **Biscotti**: Use "Biscotti" in dialogue boxes, not "Principal Biscotti".
- **Mokka**: Dry and deadpan robot (per *Magical Starsign*); speaks like a mathematician.
- **Kirsche**: Mimic Pico's speech style.
- **Cider**: Romanticist; can sound slightly Shakespearean.
- **Candy**: Valley girl speech pattern.
- **Pistachio**: Says "Meep" frequently.
- **Pyrites**: Play through *Magical Starsign* to pin down their speech style.

## Control codes

- `\85\00たち` — Currently translating as "\85\00 and the others". Consider dropping "and the others".

## Water People dialogue

Water People speech is genuine gibberish generated via a vigenère cipher with key `BUBBLE`.
End each Water People dialogue line with `, *bubble*`.
